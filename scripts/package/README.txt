1. Unzip campus-app.zip (Windows: right-click > Extract All; Mac: double-click it), then open the campus-app folder.
2. Double-click start.bat (Windows) or start.command (Mac; if it is blocked, right-click it > Open). Your browser opens the app. It needs no internet and no installation.
3. Keep the black/terminal window open while you use the app, and close it to stop.
