# Campus Compass

Campus Compass is a free, mobile-friendly app that helps LAU students find their way around campus. Explore a 3D model of the Beirut and Byblos campuses, tap any building to see what's inside, and search for the places you need.

## What you can do

- **Explore the campus in 3D.** Rotate, zoom and tap buildings, with a light and dark mode (buildings glow at night in dark mode).
- **Find places fast.** Search or browse by category: academic services, student life and support, food, study spots, sports, tech and ID services, venues, hangout spots, campus cats, parking and more.
- **See the details.** Each place shows its building, floor and room, opening hours, contact info and a link to the official LAU page.
- **Get directions.** One tap opens walking directions in Google Maps.
- **Use it anywhere.** Install it on your phone like an app, and it keeps working without internet after your first visit.

## Campuses

- **Beirut:** buildings, facilities, food, study spots, sports, venues, parking and more.
- **Byblos:** the 3D campus is ready, and its places are coming soon.
